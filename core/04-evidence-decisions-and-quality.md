# Evidence, decisions and quality control

For every material claim capture ID, statement, source (person/document/data), source date, evidence quality (`verified`, `reported`, `assumed`, `unknown`), owner and uncertainty.

For every strategic decision capture ID, decision question, options, chosen option, justification, rejected alternatives, accountable owner, decision date, status, dependencies and revisiting trigger.

## Quality review questions
- Does the strategic diagnosis distinguish symptom and root cause (Rumelt kernel)?
- Is competitive positioning specific (Porter) and is any claimed defensibility substantiated?
- Is the AI contribution explicit, and has a simpler/non-AI option been tested?
- Can value be measured from a baseline and by a deadline?
- Are affected people, ethics, data rights, security and ongoing oversight considered?
- Do operating model, workforce and technology choices enable the roadmap?
- Are assumptions, unanswered questions and decisions visible in the final report?
- Is there a coherent chain: strategic objective → problem → opportunity → investment decision → outcome → KPI?

A recommendation without evidence should be labelled provisional. A measured benefit without baseline should be labelled target/assumption, not achieved result.
