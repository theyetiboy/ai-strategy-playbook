# Workflow and stage gates

| ID | Stage | Exit decision | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Context, diagnosis and vision | Is there a sufficiently specific strategic problem? | Strategic brief |
| 2 | Opportunities and tear-down | Which opportunities are credible and why AI? | Opportunity portfolio |
| 3 | Operating model, data and technology | Can the organisation feasibly deliver? | Delivery principles and capability gaps |
| 4 | Governance, people and sustainability | Are risks controllable and oversight owned? | Risk and workforce response |
| 5 | Prioritisation and roadmap | What to fund, test, defer or reject? | Sequenced roadmap and approval gates |
| 6 | Benefits, monitoring and strategy synthesis | How will success and harm be detected? | Strategy report and next review |

At each stage: **Orient → Elicit → Challenge → Capture → Decide → Checkpoint → Continue**.

Statuses: `not_started`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `complete`, `validated` (validated requires named reviewer and date). Sections may be complete but unvalidated. Record decision confidence separately as `low`, `medium` or `high`.

A stage may be bypassed only with a documented exception and open action. Never substitute 'complete' for actual evidence or approval.

### Red flags / pause gates
- Unlawful or unjustified personal-data use; no identified lawful route to use the data.
- High-consequence automated decisions with no proportionate oversight or appeal.
- No business owner, no measurable problem, or known absence of essential data.
- Evident harm or bias with no credible mitigation.

These signal escalation, redesign, or a decision to stop; they are not automatic claims of illegality.
