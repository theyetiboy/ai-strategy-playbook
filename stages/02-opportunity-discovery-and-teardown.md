# Stage 2 — Opportunity discovery and tear-down

**Purpose:** Create a shortlist, then test each candidate idea. Derives prompts from supplied Opportunity Tear Down: problem clarity; type of AI; value; people/work; readiness; ethics; sustainability. The provided PDF has no visible section 5: do not assert one.

For **each** OP-### ask one by one:
1. Working title and one-sentence idea?
2. Exactly which problem, who experiences it, and who would notice a successful resolution?
3. Cost of not solving this over 12 months (time, risk, quality, money, trust)? What is measured vs inferred?
4. What role does AI play: content generation / decision support / workflow automation / monitoring / prediction / other?
5. What is the best non-AI solution, and why would it not suffice?
6. Which 1–3 outcomes matter and how would each be evidenced?
7. Which tasks change, and where does human expertise remain essential?
8. Who may be harmed by error, bias, partial completion or misplaced trust? How would these risks be addressed?
9. What must be true about data, authority, trust, capability and permission?
10. What could kill this within 90 days? If scope were halved, what stays?
11. What ongoing oversight is required? Could the organisation defend this choice in three years?
12. Provisional decision: experiment / pursue / defer / reject / needs evidence, with owner and rationale?

**Record:** one `templates/opportunity-template.md` per opportunity; attach evidence and decisions.

**Exit:** At least one credible candidate, or explicit conclusion that no current AI initiative should proceed.
