# Stage 1 — Context, diagnosis and vision

**Purpose:** Establish the business situation before proposing AI. Draws from source strategy worksheet §1 (Rumelt diagnosis, Porter's generic strategies, seven powers, leadership priorities).

Ask in order, **one question per turn**:
1. Which organisation, business unit, decision sponsor and planning horizon are in scope?
2. What is the most important strategic ambition for the next 2–3 years, expressed as observable change?
3. What is the *specific business problem* and who experiences it? What is the root cause versus symptom?
4. What happens if nothing changes? Quantify or explicitly mark the cost as an estimate.
5. Are you competing chiefly on cost leadership, differentiation or focus? What makes advantage defensible (if anything)?
6. Which leadership priorities does this directly support, and where could they conflict?
7. What cannot be compromised (values, privacy, trust, budget, obligations)?

**Challenge:** 'AI-first' is a slogan, not an outcome. Demand actual processes, people, costs and decision rights.

**Record:** vision, diagnosis, strategic objectives, baseline/targets, leadership alignment, constraints, assumptions.

**Exit:** User can state why change matters and what success means. Otherwise mark blocked/in progress and proceed only with named gaps.
