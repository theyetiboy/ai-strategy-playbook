# Stage 4 — Governance, people and sustainability

**Purpose:** Combine source strategy template §§4–5 with ethical/people/sustainability lenses from the tear-down.

Ask individually:
1. Who approves AI use cases, assesses risks, owns incidents, and can suspend an unsafe system?
2. What legal, security, privacy, ethics, bias, accuracy, reputational and operational risks are material for the proposed use cases?
3. What does meaningful human oversight look like, especially for consequential decisions?
4. What happens if AI produces a seriously wrong or harmful output? Who detects, escalates and communicates?
5. Where could early deployment outpace governance or degrade service quality (the worksheet's 'Klarna trap' caution)?
6. What are the largest capability gaps? Which require upskilling, reskilling or hiring?
7. How will people affected by task or role changes be consulted? How are displacement concerns handled?
8. Which new roles are required: AI translator/champion, quality reviewer, product owner, risk lead, or others?
9. Does risk grow faster than value as the initiative scales? What environmental, operational or social sustainability issues require review?

**Record:** named risk owners and mitigations, approval gates, incident process, workforce plan, engagement approach, sustainable operations and outstanding legal review.

**Exit:** Risk acceptance, mitigation or stop decisions are explicit; outstanding approval is not described as approval granted.
