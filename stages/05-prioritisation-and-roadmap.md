# Stage 5 — Prioritisation and roadmap

**Purpose:** Turn a portfolio into deliberate choices. Based on source strategy template §6.

Ask individually:
1. Which use cases most strongly serve the strategic objective? Trace each to outcome.
2. What do we know about value, feasibility, data readiness, capacity, time to value, risk and dependencies?
3. Which use case will be first and why? What evidence would reverse that decision?
4. Which ideas are explicitly excluded, rejected or deferred and why?
5. What phased actions, owners and target dates are realistic? Avoid invented dates.
6. What approvals or gates exist before live production (procurement, privacy, legal, security, finance, executive)?
7. What is the smallest meaningful pilot and what are its stop/go criteria?

**Priority method (playbook proposal):** rate strategic fit, expected benefit, feasibility and readiness 1–5 with reasons; record risk separately (low/medium/high, with escalation), rather than hiding safety concerns inside a simple weighted total. If evidence is poor, label rating provisional. Never imply scoring replaces governance gates.

**Record:** prioritisation matrix, roadmap by phase, decisions, dependencies, funding and approval needs.

**Exit:** A justified first bet, transparent non-priorities, named owners or 'to confirm', and gates.
