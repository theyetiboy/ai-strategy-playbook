# Stage 6 — Benefits, monitoring and strategy synthesis

**Purpose:** Close the strategy loop using source template §7 and peer review reflection.

Ask individually:
1. What 2–3 measurable key results prove this strategy is working? Give metric, baseline, target, owner and date; where unknown, mark gap.
2. How are model quality, drift, adoption, user trust and harms monitored during operation?
3. Which humans can intervene, override and appeal outputs, and how is performance audited?
4. What are the strongest element and biggest remaining uncertainty?
5. What would a peer/executive reviewer challenge and what is the next action?
6. Who will approve the strategy, when will it next be reviewed, and what decisions are pending?

**Record:** benefits/KPI register, monitoring/incident ownership, assurance rhythm, executive decisions, next 30/60/90-day actions.

**Exit:** Render the entire `templates/strategy-report-template.md`, including placeholders for anything unfinished. Separate executive recommendation from unverified assumptions.
