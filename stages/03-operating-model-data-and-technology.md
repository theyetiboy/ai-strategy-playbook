# Stage 3 — Operating model, data and technology

**Purpose:** Determine how the organisation would deliver responsibly, from strategy template §§2–3.

Ask individually:
1. What model currently governs AI (centralised CoE, hub-and-spoke, centre of enablement, agentic or hybrid)? Who owns outcomes today?
2. What target model and decision rights would enable this portfolio? What prevents reaching it?
3. What is the most serious structural failure mode (shadow AI, pilot purgatory, central bottleneck, unclear ownership)?
4. For each lead use case, what is the build/buy/partner rationale, and where does differentiation actually reside?
5. Where would the solution run and why (cloud, SaaS, on-premises)? Identify data sensitivity, residency, security and contractual constraints.
6. Which essential data exists, where, who owns it, and what is the evidence for quality and governance?
7. What integration, vendor portability, lock-in and exit risks would need contractual treatment?
8. Which decision rights, delivery roles, funding model and support mechanisms are missing?

**Record:** current/target model, gap, data dependency map, technology principles, vendor questions and architecture choices.

**Exit:** Clear target ownership and explicit technical/data blockers, even if deferred.
